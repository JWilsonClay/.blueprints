## 2. Dynamic Observations

### The "Logic Echo" Effect
Protocols like `OP-MANUAL-REPAIR` and `OP-PRECISION-PRECISE` are currently "Logic Echoes" of each other. They share the same surgical philosophy (atomicity, diff-focus) but live in separate files. This increases token overhead without adding unique constraints.

### The "Anchor Dependency" Pattern
Robustness in the substrate follows a gravity-like model:
- **Primary Anchors** (`RISK-AUDIT`, `SUBSTRATE-COMP`) exert the most influence.
- **Secondary Protocols** (`BUILD`, `ASSIMILATE`) inherited their rules (e.g., Ventilated Prose) via explicit reference.
- **Surface Protocols** (`REPAIR`, `TERMINAL`) are functional but lack the recursive self-auditing depth of the anchors.
